## Design Context

### Users
- **Fund manager / trader (primary).** A single expert operator running a real-assets fund (**Real Assets Dashboard**) across precious metals (gold, silver, platinum, palladium), industrial metals (copper) and digital assets (bitcoin): physically backed and spot ETFs (GLD, IAU, SLV, PHYS, GDX…), listed futures (COMEX/NYMEX/CME: GC, SI, HG, PL, PA, BTC outrights, spreads and butterflies), allocated physical bullion and custody balances. They use it daily to decide allocation, spot relative-value trades, read the macro regime and record every entry and exit. They are quantitatively literate and distrust black boxes.
- **Investors / LPs (secondary).** They get a read-only factsheet: NAV per unit, performance, allocation, risk and commentary. It is often printed or exported to PDF.

### Brand personality
- **Assured, precise, discreet.**
- **Emotional goal:** the calm confidence of a private-bank report. Confidence comes from transparency (sources, dates, breakdowns), not from adjectives or decoration.
- **Voice:** sober and quantitative. It says what is known, what is modeled and what is uncertain.

### Aesthetic direction
- **Hybrid.**
  - The shell, Overview, Portfolio and the Investor view are editorial and refined: generous rhythm, a serif display face for headlines and NAV figures, restrained ink/charcoal surfaces.
  - The Quant Lab, Markets, Macro and Data Center are terminal-grade: dense tables, compact type, tabular mono numerals, many small panels.
- **Theme:** dark by default for trading, and a first-class light theme for reading and printing (the Investor view defaults to light). Both themes use the same token names.
- **Colour:** gold is a *signal*, not a wallpaper. A single desaturated gold brand hue marks focus, NAV and the primary series. Gains and losses use a semantic pos/neg pair; tiers use semantic colours; everything else is neutral ink with a slight warm tint.
  - **Asset colours** (`--asset-<id>`) identify an asset's series in charts only, never as fills or backgrounds. They are restrained, mid-chroma hues, distinct from the brand gold and the pos/neg pair in light, dark and print: gold, a cool silver grey, a red-brown copper, a dark cool platinum slate, a light warm palladium grey and a muted bitcoin orange. Each asset is also named in text wherever its colour appears.
- **Wordmark:** a serif "Real" with small-caps "Assets Dashboard"; the favicon is a plain serif monogram on ink.
- **Anti-references:**
  - crypto/casino neon (glows, gradients, gamified hype), even on the bitcoin views;
  - generic SaaS templates (purple gradients, an icon on every card, Inter everywhere, identical card grids);
  - gold kitsch (metallic gradients, bullion clip-art, yellow floods).

### Design principles
1. **Real data or an explicit empty state.** Never show a fake-looking placeholder. Modeled figures carry a visible "Modeled" chip and their source.
2. **Every number can be broken down.** Scores show their drivers, NAV shows its sleeves, P&L shows its lots. Every panel shows its provenance ("Source · data through [date]").
3. **Caveats are first-class UI.** Validation status (passed, failed, untested), data freshness and key-missing states appear next to the number, not in a footnote.
4. **Dense but calm.** Hierarchy comes from type scale and spacing, not boxes and colour. Numbers are tabular and right-aligned; labels are small caps.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG AA contrast in both themes; colour is never the only signal (chips carry text); `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected; everything is reachable by keyboard.
